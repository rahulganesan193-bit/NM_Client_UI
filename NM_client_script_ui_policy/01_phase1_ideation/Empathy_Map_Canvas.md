## Empathy Map Canvas

An empathy map is a simple, easy-to-digest visual that captures knowledge about a user's behaviours and attitudes.

It is a useful tool to help teams better understand their users. Creating an effective solution requires understanding the true problem and the person who is experiencing it. The exercise of creating the map helps participants consider things from the user's perspective along with his or her goals and challenges.

### Empathy Map – Service Desk Agent / Fulfiller (Incident Management)

| Quadrant | Notes |
|---|---|
| **THINK & FEEL** | • I need to log this fast<br>• Is this the right category?<br>• Will the resolver understand my ticket?<br>• Pressured by queue and SLA |
| **HEAR** | • Resolver: "This ticket has no details"<br>• Manager: "Close tickets within SLA"<br>• Caller: "When will it be fixed?" |
| **SEE** | • Form with many optional fields<br>• Tickets reopened / reassigned<br>• Inconsistent categories in reports |
| **SAY & DO** | • Types short, vague descriptions<br>• Skips optional fields<br>• Picks the first category<br>• Calls caller back for details |
